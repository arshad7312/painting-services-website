A few good questions

Before the first brushstroke.

Have another question? We’re happy to talk it through.

Ask us about your project ↗
How do I know which colours will work in my home?

Light, room size and the way you use a space all matter. We’ll talk through your ideas and help you explore colours and finishes during your consultation.

Can I get an estimate before deciding?

Absolutely. Share a few details and we’ll arrange a conversation and a home assessment before preparing a project-specific estimate. There’s no obligation to proceed.

Will I need to move all my furniture?

We’ll explain what needs to be moved or covered before work begins. Our team takes care to protect the space and agrees on preparation with you in advance.

Which areas do you serve?

We paint homes across Bengaluru, including Vijinapura, KR Puram, Whitefield, Hebbal, Yelahanka, Indiranagar, Marathahalli, HSR Layout, Electronic City and Jayanagar. Share your neighbourhood on WhatsApp and we will confirm availability.

How much advance do I need to pay?

We take 35% advance to confirm your booking and schedule the crew and materials. The balance is paid after the work is completed to your satisfaction.

How much does house painting cost in Bengaluru?

Interior repainting with Asian Paints material starts from ₹12 per sq ft, and fresh painting costs ₹5 more per sq ft. Use our free quotation builder for an itemised estimate with GST. The final price is confirmed after a site visit.

Do you provide waterproofing and crack repair?

Yes. We treat damp, seepage, ceiling cracks, peeling paint and terrace or sheet leaks at the source before painting, so the finish lasts. See the Problems we repair and Leakage proofing galleries for real examples.

Which paint brands do you use?

We use Asian Paints — Tractor Emulsion, Apcolite and the Royale range inside, and Ace, Apex and Ultima Protek outside. Your quotation lists the exact product, area and rate per sq ft.

How long will the project take?

It depends on the size of your home, the surfaces and the finish you choose. We’ll give you a clear schedule with your estimate before the project starts.
